Nitrogen dioxide at ground level across the United States

This dataset provides estimates of monthly and annual average surface-level nitrogen dioxide concentrations across the continental United States.

The estimates are derived using a land-use regression that integrates TROPOMI satellite observations with land-use data from OpenStreetMap and Google Earth Engine and meteorological reanalysis data from ERA5.

The dataset is freely available on Zenodo.

Download the data This is v2.US.D.01.

Citation for the related paper:

Nawaz, M. O.; Goldberg, D. L.; Kerr, G. H.; Anenberg, S. C. TROPOMI satellite data reshape NO2 air pollution land-use regression modeling capabilities in the United States. ACS ES&T Air 2025, 2 (2), 187–200. https://doi.org/10.1021/acsestair.4c00153
