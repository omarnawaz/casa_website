Everyone is welcome in CASA.

I am committed to maintaining an inclusive and respectful research environment within CASA and within the School of Physical, Chemical and Environmental Sciences at Cardiff University, where I serve as Equality, Diversity, Inclusion and Anti‑Racism Lead.

Prospective PhD students

I am keen to mentor PhD students interested in understanding how human activities affect climate and air quality, and in using this knowledge to improve health and progress towards sustainability. You will learn atmospheric modelling on high-performance computing systems, satellite remote sensing applications, and methods for quantifying the health and equity impacts of pollution and climate change. My priority is mentoring your scientific writing, presenting and research skills while tailoring your PhD towards your career goals. Further details are available on Cardiff University’s postgraduate research pages.

Prospective postdoctoral researchers

My focus when working with postdoctoral researchers is career development, including feedback on scientific writing, presentations and research. Whether your next step is in research, lecturing or outside academia, I am committed to supporting it, and I am keen to collaborate on fellowship proposals.

Masters and undergraduate students

If you are interested in exploring how climate change, air pollution, health, policy and equity interact, please get in touch. I provide guidance on scientific research and writing, and support students in learning high-performance computing, data analysis and visualisation, and scientific communication. Depending on your interests, projects can span climate science, earth system modelling, remote sensing, data science, machine learning and environmental justice.

The group wiki contains our code of conduct, getting-started guides and documentation on the models and data we use.
