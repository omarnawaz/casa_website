Publications

This page lists my peer-reviewed publications, as lead author and as co‑author.

Selected publications

  • National climate action can ameliorate, perpetuate, or exacerbate international air pollution inequalities

    Nature Communications, 2026

    This paper sets out a health modelling framework to quantify inequality in how air pollution health impacts are exchanged between countries under different climate futures, and assesses the resulting disparities between the Global North and South.

    Read the paper

  • Burden of 375 diseases and injuries, risk-attributable burden of 88 risk factors, and healthy life expectancy in 204 countries and territories: a systematic analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023

    The Lancet, 2025

    For the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023, I developed the nitrogen dioxide exposure dataset and worked with other public health experts to assess the years lived with disability from childhood asthma globally since 1990.

    Read the paper

  • A source apportionment and emission scenario assessment of PM2.5- and O3-related health impacts in G20 countries

    GeoHealth, 2023

    This paper contrasts the contributions of domestic and transboundary sectoral sources to national air pollution health burdens in G20 countries, and projects the public health co-benefits of achieving net-zero CO2 targets.

    Read the paper
